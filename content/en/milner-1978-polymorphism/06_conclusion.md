We have presented a discipline of polymorphic programming which appears to be practically useful, and have given a rather simple type-checking algorithm. In a restricted language we have shown that this algorithm can be proved correct (the proof was factored into two Soundness Theorems). Though much work remains to be done, we hope to have made the point that the practice of type checking can and should by supported by semantic theory and proof.
