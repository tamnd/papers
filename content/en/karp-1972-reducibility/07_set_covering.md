INPUT: finite family of finite sets $\{ S_j \}$, positive integer k
PROPERTY: There is a subfamily $\{ T_h \} \subseteq \{ S_j \}$ containing $\leq k$ sets such that $\bigcup_{h} T_h = \bigcup_{j} S_j$.
