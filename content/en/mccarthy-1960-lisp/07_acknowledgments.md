The inadequacy of the $\lambda$-notation for naming recursive functions was noticed by N. Rochester, and he discovered an alternative to the solution involving *label* which has been used here. The form of subroutine for *cons* which permits its composition with other functions was invented, in connection with another programming system, by C. Gerberick and H. L. Gelernter, of IBM Corporation. The LlSP programming system was developed by a group including R. Brayton, D. Edwards, P. Fox, L. Hodes, D. Luckham, K. Maling, J. McCarthy, D. Park, S. Russell.

The group was supported by the M.I.T. Computation Center, and by the M.I.T. Research Laboratory of Electronics (which is supported in part by the the U.S. Army (Signal Corps), the U.S. Air Force (Office of Scientific Research, Air Research and Development Command), and the U.S. Navy (Office of Naval Research)). The author also wishes to acknowledge the personal financial support of the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation.
